Computing Techniques that Amplify Human Skills

As AI and technology as a whole continues to improve and advance more tools and techniques are used against cybersecurity professionals. These professionals are what stand between the threat and vulnerable systems and data. Since tools and techniques are being used to create threats then similar tools can be use to fight back. The techniques that I am interested in are AI and algorithims. The cooperation between people and machine is what we should strive for, rather than full replacement the aplification of human skill is what I am interested in.

AI In Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity teams deal with many threats to data and systems. Like many things AI has stepped in and taken over some aspects of the everyday duties of a cybersecurity analyst. As Microsoft's 2026 Digital Defense Report points out security analysts have to maintain lots of systems but those attacking the systems only need one entry point to cause damage. The same way that AI is being used to amplifying attacks at machine speeds, AI should be used to defend systems at machine speeds. One way that cybersecurity analysts have been using AI according to the Google Threat Intelligence's Cybersecurity Forecast 2026 is that they correlate date, write summaries, and draft threat intelligence from the attacks recieved. This is something that google calls "Agentic SOC", the endless alerts that analysts recieve everyday have now been redirected to AI and this keeps them sorting through alerts the whole day to now being able to focus on bigger threats and being able to interpret data/results at a higher level. In this instance the skill being amplified is our attention span and speed.

The age of these two sources indicate that these are recent developments in the world of cybersecurity. The Google forecast was released in November of last year and the Microsoft defense report is was released the first of October 2026. Apart from the publishing date the Google Cybersecurity forecast has language that indicates it was being implimented a year ago and like it says these aren't "crystal ball predictions" they use trends and data they observe. This computing technique connects back to the human-computer cooperation idea from Shyam Sankar's TED talk. The technology matters less by itself and it is more valuable when others like cybersecurity analysts use it.

Algorithims Use In Cybersecurity

When I hear the word algorithims my mind goes straight to talks about the Youtube or Tiktok algorithims and how they keep people scrolling or they recommend video for our enjoyment. However, using algorithims in cybersecurity via the Fuzzing technique is interesting and something I knew very little about. As the OWASP article explains fuzzing as a technique that is used to tests software with the intent of looking for bugs, vulnerabilities or oddities in the way it runs. Into the program or Fuzzer goes semi-random data and the program is monitored for issues. The human skill being amplified here through human-computer cooperation are testing skills and time. Like attacks we spoke about before, a person can try so many inputs by hand. A fuzzer though can keep trying inputs and generating new ones to find issues.

Circling back to AI for a second the a 2024 systematic review in Computers & Security connects AI and the Fuzzing computing technique. The traditional technique of fuzzing was developed back in 1988, but it has evolved since then. The systemic review touches on how machine learning is being used to overcome some issues that traditional fuzzer technique encounter like lack of strategy, deep bug identification, time sensitive analysis, quality of inputs, just to name a few.
